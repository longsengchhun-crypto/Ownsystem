About Vanguard Creative

Founded in Phnom Penh in 2022, Vanguard Creative has quickly emerged as one of Southeast Asia's premium visual production firms. We empower brands by generating cinema-grade videos, striking commercial photography, tailored visual guidelines, and multi-cam livestream setups.

We believe the path to high-end content should be simple and structured. By merging specialized creative professionals with our integrated client portal software, we provide clear timelines, transparent scope quotations, and high-fidelity project assets in one seamless ledger.
